## Summary

<div class="bi" markdown>
<div class="bi-en" markdown>

**The paper presents a Samsung/Siemens EDA methodology for validating ISO 26262 fault-campaign stimulus and setup (XPROP=resolve, race and delay handling, full state-element good-machine checks, safety-mechanism activation, fault-list reduction), showing good-machine deviations falling from about 15% to 4.63% on a Samsung design.**

</div>
<div class="bi-ko" markdown>

**본 논문은 ISO 26262 fault campaign의 stimulus 및 setup 품질을 검증하는 Samsung/Siemens EDA 방법론(XPROP=resolve, race 및 delay 처리, 전체 state element 대상 good-machine 점검, safety mechanism 활성화, fault list 축소)을 제시하며, Samsung 설계에서 good-machine deviation이 약 15%에서 4.63%로 감소함을 보입니다.**

</div>
</div>

## Key Insights

<div class="bi" markdown>
<div class="bi-en" markdown>

- Stimulus generated with XPROP=noXprop left about 15% of state elements deviating in good-machine simulation; switching to XPROP=resolve reduced this to 4.63%.

</div>
<div class="bi-ko" markdown>

- XPROP=noXprop으로 생성한 stimulus는 good-machine simulation에서 state element의 약 15%를 deviation 상태로 남겼으며, XPROP=resolve로 전환하자 4.63%로 감소했습니다.

</div>
</div>

<div class="bi" markdown>
<div class="bi-en" markdown>

- Good-machine checking must compare all state elements (flip-flops, latches) and memory contents, not only observe points, to catch dormant deviations that would otherwise corrupt fault results.

</div>
<div class="bi-ko" markdown>

- Good-machine 점검은 observe point뿐 아니라 모든 state element(flip-flop, latch)와 memory 내용을 비교해야 잠복 deviation을 잡아 잘못된 fault 결과를 막을 수 있습니다.

</div>
</div>

<div class="bi" markdown>
<div class="bi-en" markdown>

- Of the 37,546 residual good-machine deviations, 27,205 came from reset-to-clock races, 7,722 from RTL # delays, 1,845 from simulation/synthesis mismatches (FIFO out-of-index), 770 from force-list issues, and 4 from parameter mismatches.

</div>
<div class="bi-ko" markdown>

- 잔여 good-machine deviation 37,546건 중 27,205건은 reset-to-clock race, 7,722건은 RTL # delay, 1,845건은 simulation/synthesis mismatch(FIFO out-of-index), 770건은 force list 문제, 4건은 parameter 불일치에서 비롯되었습니다.

</div>
</div>

<div class="bi" markdown>
<div class="bi-en" markdown>

- Safety mechanisms must be activated (error-injection self-check) before a campaign; otherwise every fault is marked undetected, and stimulus that cannot inject a fault shows up as Not Injected (9.38% in the 4,598-fault statistical sample).

</div>
<div class="bi-ko" markdown>

- Campaign 전에 safety mechanism을 error injection 자체 점검으로 활성화해야 하며, 그렇지 않으면 모든 fault가 undetected로 표시되고 stimulus가 주입하지 못한 fault는 Not Injected(4,598 fault 통계 샘플에서 9.38%)로 분류됩니다.

</div>
</div>

<div class="bi" markdown>
<div class="bi-en" markdown>

- In the 4,598-fault statistical sample, 65.87% of faults were alarm-detected and 9.86% were residual; the NOC in Safety Island detection ratio rose to 65.87% from 42.81% reported in the prior DVCon paper.

</div>
<div class="bi-ko" markdown>

- 4,598 fault 통계 샘플에서 65.87%가 alarm-detected, 9.86%가 residual로 나타났으며, NOC in Safety Island의 검출 비율은 이전 DVCon 논문의 42.81%에서 65.87%로 상승했습니다.

</div>
</div>

## Detailed Summary

### Background and Problem

<div class="bi" markdown>
<div class="bi-en" markdown>

- Fault campaigns for ISO 26262 on large automotive SoCs are costly; the authors' prior DVCon US 2023 paper covered the overall flow but not how to verify that campaign results are accurate.

</div>
<div class="bi-ko" markdown>

- 대형 automotive SoC에서 ISO 26262용 fault campaign은 비용이 크며, 저자들의 이전 DVCon US 2023 논문은 전체 flow는 다루었으나 campaign 결과의 정확성 검증 방법은 다루지 않았습니다.

</div>
</div>

<div class="bi" markdown>
<div class="bi-en" markdown>

- Open questions: whether stimulus reflects real use cases, whether RTL-based stimulus matches gate-level results, how to accelerate campaigns, and when good-machine simulation is required.

</div>
<div class="bi-ko" markdown>

- 주요 질문: stimulus가 실제 use case를 반영하는지, RTL 기반 stimulus가 gate-level 결과와 일치하는지, campaign을 어떻게 가속할지, good-machine simulation이 언제 필요한지.

</div>
</div>

### Architecture and Methodology

<div class="bi" markdown>
<div class="bi-en" markdown>

- Stimulus quality: identical design setup (file lists, defines, parameters, memory preloads); testbench forces must propagate across all hierarchy; avoid RTL `#` delays; use lint-clean RTL; use XPROP=resolve rather than X-optimism.

</div>
<div class="bi-ko" markdown>

- Stimulus 품질: 설계 setup(file list, define, parameter, memory preload)을 동일하게 유지하고, testbench force는 모든 hierarchy를 따라 전파되어야 하며, RTL `#` delay를 피하고, lint-clean RTL을 사용하며, X-optimism 대신 XPROP=resolve를 사용합니다.

</div>
</div>

<div class="bi" markdown>
<div class="bi-en" markdown>

- Races: avoid reset-to-clock and data-to-clock races (KaleidoScope reset-delay feature; QuestaSim `-deraceclockdata`).

</div>
<div class="bi-ko" markdown>

- Race: reset-to-clock 및 data-to-clock race를 피합니다(KaleidoScope reset delay 기능, QuestaSim `-deraceclockdata`).

</div>
</div>

<div class="bi" markdown>
<div class="bi-en" markdown>

- Good-machine simulation compares observe points, all state elements, and memory contents against the stimulus to expose dormant deviations.

</div>
<div class="bi-ko" markdown>

- Good-machine simulation은 observe point뿐 아니라 모든 state element와 memory 내용을 stimulus 기준과 비교하여 잠복 deviation을 드러냅니다.

</div>
</div>

<div class="bi" markdown>
<div class="bi-en" markdown>

- Fault-list reduction: safety-mechanism-aware COI pruning, fault collapsing, statistical random sampling; safety mechanisms must be exercised via error injection and stimulus graded by toggle or propagation.

</div>
<div class="bi-ko" markdown>

- Fault list 축소: safety-mechanism-aware COI pruning, fault collapsing, statistical random sampling. Safety mechanism은 error injection으로 동작시켜야 하며, stimulus는 toggle 또는 propagation 기준으로 grading합니다.

</div>
</div>

### Results and Conclusions

<div class="bi" markdown>
<div class="bi-en" markdown>

- Good-machine deviation dropped from about 15% (XPROP=noXprop) to 4.63% (XPROP=resolve); remaining 37,546 deviations were attributed to races, RTL delays, sim/synth mismatches, force lists, and parameters.

</div>
<div class="bi-ko" markdown>

- Good-machine deviation은 약 15%(XPROP=noXprop)에서 4.63%(XPROP=resolve)로 감소했으며, 남은 37,546건의 deviation은 race, RTL delay, sim/synth mismatch, force list, parameter 문제에 기인한 것으로 분석되었습니다.

</div>
</div>

<div class="bi" markdown>
<div class="bi-en" markdown>

- A 4,598-fault statistical sample gave 65.87% alarm-detected, 14.98% no deviation, 9.86% residual, 9.38% not injected.

</div>
<div class="bi-ko" markdown>

- 4,598 fault 통계 샘플 결과: alarm-detected 65.87%, no deviation 14.98%, residual 9.86%, not injected 9.38%.

</div>
</div>

<div class="bi" markdown>
<div class="bi-en" markdown>

- The NOC in Safety Island detected ratio rose to 65.87% from 42.81% in the prior paper's best configuration.

</div>
<div class="bi-ko" markdown>

- NOC in Safety Island의 검출 비율은 이전 논문의 최고 구성 42.81%에서 65.87%로 상승했습니다.

</div>
</div>

<div class="bi" markdown>
<div class="bi-en" markdown>

- Limitation: single Samsung design; several authors are tool developers, and claims such as results being "always accurate" are not independently validated.

</div>
<div class="bi-ko" markdown>

- 한계: 단일 Samsung 설계이며, 다수 저자가 도구 개발자이고, 결과가 "항상 정확하다"는 주장은 독립적으로 검증되지 않았습니다.

</div>
</div>
